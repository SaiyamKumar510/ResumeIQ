Data Analyst

Our growth team is hiring a Data Analyst to turn raw data into decisions.

Responsibilities
- Write SQL queries to extract and analyse data from MySQL and PostgreSQL databases.
- Perform data cleaning and data analysis in Python using Pandas and NumPy.
- Build dashboards in Tableau or Power BI and present insights to stakeholders.
- Apply statistics to measure the impact of product changes, including A/B testing.
- Create charts with Matplotlib for ad-hoc reports and use Excel for quick analysis.
- Help maintain ETL pipelines that feed our reporting tables.

Requirements
- 1+ years of experience in an analytics role.
- Strong SQL and Excel skills.
- Ability to communicate findings clearly to non-technical colleagues.
